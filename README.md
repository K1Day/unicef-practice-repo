# Almaty Price Tracker 🛒

Almaty Price Tracker is a simple web app that helps people in Almaty compare grocery prices in different stores and save money on everyday shopping.

## The problem
Food prices in Almaty change often and are different in every store. To find the best price, people have to visit several shops or check many websites. Students and families with a limited budget often end up paying more than they need to.

## Our solution
Almaty Price Tracker collects prices of common products in one place, so anyone can quickly see where a product is cheapest today and how its price has changed over time.

## Main features
- **Price comparison**: see the price of one product in different stores side by side
- **Cheapest store finder**: find the store with the lowest price for your shopping list
- **Price history**: simple charts that show how prices change by week and month
- **Community updates**: users can add or update a price they saw in a store
- **Favorites**: save products you buy often and get a quick overview of their prices

## Products and stores
The first version focuses on everyday products, such as bread, milk, eggs, rice, flour, sugar, chicken, vegetables and fruit.

Planned stores include large supermarket chains and local markets in Almaty.

## Who it is for
- Students living on a budget
- Families planning their weekly shopping
- Anyone in Almaty who wants to spend less on groceries
