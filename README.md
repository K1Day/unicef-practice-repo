# Almaty Price Tracker

Almaty Price Tracker is a simple web app that helps people in Almaty compare grocery prices in different stores.

## Problem
Food prices in Almaty change often and are different in every store. People spend a lot of time checking prices or end up paying more than they need to.

## What the app does
- Shows prices of common products (bread, milk, eggs, rice and more) in popular stores
- Lets users compare prices and find the cheapest store nearby
- Shows how prices change over time with simple charts
- Allows users to add or update prices they saw in a store

## Who it is for
Students, families and anyone in Almaty who wants to save money on groceries.
